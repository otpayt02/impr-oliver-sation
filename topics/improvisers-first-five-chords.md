## **The 10-Minute Improvisation Plan**

Human in the loop: this part is yours.

> > > 1. **Listen (2 min).** Tap all five cards above. Don't play yet — just hear how each one feels. Notice you already have favorites. That taste is your style forming.  
> > > 2. ---

> > >    **Land the shapes (3 min).** Play each voicing slowly, left hand first, then right. Dashed pills go in your left hand. Let the pedal blur everything together.  
> > > 3. ---

> > >    **Run the loop (2 min).** Play C add9 → G9sus → Am(add9) → Fmaj7 around and around, two beats per chord, until you don't have to look.  
> > > 4. ---

> > >    **Improvise (2 min).** Keep the loop going with your left hand \+ one right-hand finger. Noodle only on the A minor pentatonic notes: A, C, D, E, G. Every single note works over every chord. You cannot sound wrong. This is the cheat code.  
> > > 5. ---

> > >    **Capture it (1 min).** Hit voice-memo record on your phone and play the loop with pentatonic noodles for 60 seconds. Congratulations — that recording is your first real improvisation.
