# AP Music & Audio — minimal landing specification

September 8, 2026. This supersedes the six-chapter landing; the cream services, collaborator preference, examples, local brief, and TEST checkout remain.

## Job
Help a visitor recognize a useful music or audio outcome, then enter the service catalog or start a conversation. The first viewport contains only identity, one complete service sentence, the signal composition, and three service routes.

## Choreography
Desktop runway: 260svh, native scroll, sticky viewport. Five phase-shifted, burst-modulated waves begin with irregular left-side HTML/hash fragments. Horizontal position and scroll progress smoothly resolve the same geometry into low-frequency waves and sparse notation. No scroll hijacking. Canvas capped at 120 entities, DPR 2, approximately 30 fps; hidden/offscreen animation pauses.

Headline: twelve curated whole sentences, 5.8-second cadence, previous six excluded. Unique verbs, objects, and endings prevent partial repetition. A quiet 450ms opacity/8px reveal identifies changes; fixed line structure keeps layout stable. Pause and Next are keyboard controls. Reduced motion uses a static resolved scene with manual phrase selection. Short mobile viewports use natural page height.

## Content and conversion boundaries
Three routes: Create & record, Learn & refine, Perform & be heard. Detailed Music/Audio columns and Oliver Payton/Alexander Say selection follow in cream sections. Existing process samples are illustrative, not invented client proof. Requests are local prepared briefs; no AI completion claim or unconditional delivery promise. Google Pay and checkout remain TEST, not settlement.

Audience changes and live payment settlement are outside this release.
