What we do

We hear it. We write it. You play it.

Ear-Driven Transcription

Send us any recording — a voice memo, a live set, a YouTube link. We listen, we notate. You get accurate sheet music and chord charts, in your key.

Arrangement

Piano reductions, full-band charts, choir parts, solo-instrument adaptations. We shape the piece for the players you actually have.

Improvisation Coaching

One-on-one and small-group lessons in piano, theory, and the craft of thinking in real time. From first chord changes to advanced language.

Audio Production

Recording, editing, mixing, and cleanup for performances, demos, and worship sets. Polished sound, fast turnaround.

The philosophy

“Improvisation isn’t the absence of a plan — it’s listening faster than the music.”

As practice

Every transcription and arrangement starts the way our own playing does: by ear, on purpose, then confirmed on the page. The chart you receive has been played before it was printed.

As philosophy

A written chart and a real-time solo are the same skill viewed at different speeds. That belief shapes how we teach, arrange, and deliver.
