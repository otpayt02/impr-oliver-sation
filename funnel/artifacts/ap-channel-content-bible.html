Soft Violence — Avant-garde alt-metal for deep work

Not wall-to-wall heavy. Open with fragile ambience — felt piano, clean guitar, huge reverb — then let sudden low-tuned progressive-djent riffs interrupt the beauty, and return to softness again. The contrast is the hook: dynamic motion in the spirit of Sleep Token's quiet-to-eruption architecture, without imitating any artist's sound. Instrumental. Human-curated.

SUNO STYLE PROMPT (instrumental): Instrumental alternative metal / progressive metal with avant-garde atmosphere. Begin intimate and soft: sparse felt piano, ambient clean guitar, huge reverb, vulnerable cinematic space. Build patiently into sudden low-tuned syncopated progressive-djent guitar riffs, polyrhythmic drums, controlled double-kick impact. Alternate quiet beautiful breakdowns with heavy precise releases. Sophisticated harmony, unusual textures, evolving long-form arrangement, 92–110 BPM, dark but hopeful, polished modern production. No vocals. No artist imitation. Designed for deep focus.
